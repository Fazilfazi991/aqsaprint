Why Safety Signage Is Non-Negotiable

Construction sites, warehouses, and industrial facilities are legally required to display clear safety warnings and traffic control signs. Beyond compliance, visible signage prevents accidents, saves lives, and protects your business from liability.

AQSA Print manufactures reflective safety signs using Grade 1 or Grade 2 reflective sheeting on weatherproof aluminum substrates. All signs meet KSA safety regulations and are tested for durability in harsh outdoor conditions.

FAQ

Common Questions

Are your signs compliant with KSA regulations?

Yes. All our traffic and safety signs meet Saudi standards for color, symbols, reflectivity, and bilingual text where required.

What reflectivity grade should I choose?

Grade 1 (engineer-grade) is suitable for low-traffic areas. Grade 2 (high-intensity) is recommended for highways and high-speed zones. We can advise based on your application.

Can you produce custom safety signs?

Absolutely. Share your design, text, and dimensions and we'll manufacture custom safety signs to your exact requirements.

How long do reflective signs last outdoors?

With proper installation, our reflective safety signs last 5-10 years in Riyadh's climate. UV lamination protects against fading and cracking.

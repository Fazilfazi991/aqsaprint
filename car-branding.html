FAQ

Common Questions About Car Branding

Will the vinyl damage my car's paint?

No — quality cast vinyl actually protects your original paint from UV rays and minor abrasions. When removed correctly, it leaves zero residue and the paint underneath is preserved in original condition.

How long does a car wrap last?

Our premium cast vinyl lasts 3–5 years with normal use. Longevity depends on how the vehicle is maintained — hand washing and parking in shade significantly extends the life of the wrap.

Can I wash my car normally after branding?

Yes, but we recommend hand washing over automated brushed car washes for the first few weeks. After the vinyl has fully cured, normal washing is fine. Avoid high-pressure jets directed at wrap edges.

How many days does installation take?

A partial wrap or decal job takes a few hours. A full car wrap is typically done in 1–2 days. We'll give you an exact timeline based on your vehicle and the scope of work.

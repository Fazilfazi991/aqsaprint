FAQ

Common Printing Questions

What's the difference between digital and offset?

Digital is best for short runs (under 1,000) with fast turnaround. Offset is more cost-effective for large volumes (1,000+) and offers Pantone colour matching.

What file format should I send?

PDF is preferred. We also accept AI, EPS, PSD and high-resolution JPG/PNG (300 DPI minimum). We can convert most formats.

Do you offer design services?

Yes. If you don't have artwork ready, our in-house design team can create it. Design fees are quoted separately based on complexity.

What's the fastest turnaround?

Digital prints can be ready same-day for standard items. Offset typically takes 3–5 working days. Rush orders are available at an additional fee.

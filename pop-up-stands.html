The Ultimate Trade Show Solution

When you attend an exhibition or trade show, your display is your entire storefront. Pop-up stands offer the perfect balance between massive visual impact and complete portability.

The system utilizes a collapsible aluminum frame that "pops up" and locks into place instantly. High-resolution printed panels are then attached via magnetic strips or tensioned fabric, creating a massive, seamless backdrop that completely transforms your event space.

  • Curved or straight frame configurations to fit your booth space
  • High-resolution, anti-scratch and anti-glare printed panels
  • Entire system packs down into a single wheeled transit case
  • Hardware can be reused for years — simply order new graphic panels
System Types

Display Technologies

Choose the system that best fits your workflow and aesthetic.

Magnetic Panel Pop-ups

The classic system. Uses rigid PVC panels that attach seamlessly to the aluminum frame via magnetic strips. Offers the sharpest, most vibrant photo quality.

Tension Fabric Displays

A modern alternative. A single, large dye-sublimated fabric 'pillowcase' slides over a tubular aluminum frame and zips tight for a smooth, matte finish.

Illuminated Backdrops

Next-generation LED backlit pop-ups. Internal lighting modules illuminate your tension fabric graphic from behind, making your booth glow brilliantly.

Counter Conversions

The wheeled transit case used to transport the frame can be wrapped in a custom graphic and fitted with a wooden top to become your reception counter.

Modular & Expandable

Our pop-up systems are measured in "quads" (e.g., 3x3 or 3x4). A 3x3 curved stand is the industry standard and perfectly fits a 3-meter exhibition booth. A 3x4 provides a wider backdrop for larger spaces.

Because the hardware is modular, you aren't locked into one size. You can easily connect multiple frames together to create massive continuous walls, L-shapes, or S-curves to fit whatever space you book for your next event.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need tools to assemble it?

No tools are required whatsoever. The aluminum frame expands and locks with magnetic or snap hubs, and the graphic panels simply snap into place using magnetic strips or zippers.

Can I transport it on a plane?

Yes. The entire system (frame, magnetic bars, lights, and graphic panels) fits securely into the hard-shell wheeled case, which can be checked as standard oversized luggage on most airlines.

What happens if I damage a graphic panel?

Because the system is modular, if you accidentally crease or damage one panel, we can reprint just that specific panel to match the rest of your set, saving you from buying a whole new system.

Does it come with lights?

Our premium pop-up packages include 2 high-power LED halogen-style spotlights that clip onto the top of the frame to brightly illuminate your graphics.

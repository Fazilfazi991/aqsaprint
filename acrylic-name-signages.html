FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between engraved and printed acrylic?

Engraving creates a permanent etched effect with depth and texture. UV printing offers full-color logos and gradients. Many clients choose both — engraving for text and UV printing for logos.

Can acrylic signs be used outdoors?

Yes, acrylic is naturally UV-resistant. For outdoor use, we recommend thicker acrylic (15mm+) and UV-stabilized inks. However, for harsh outdoor environments, metal or composite materials may be better.

How do I clean acrylic signs?

Simply wipe with a soft, damp microfiber cloth. Avoid ammonia-based cleaners (like Windex) as they can damage the surface over time. We provide care instructions with every order.

Can you match my brand font?

Absolutely. Send us your brand guidelines or font files, and we'll use your exact typefaces. If you don't have them, our designers can recommend fonts that complement your brand.

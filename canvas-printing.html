FAQ

Common Questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. One of the main advantages of digital printing is that you can print as little as a single copy. It is highly cost-effective for short runs.

How fast can you print my order?

Many digital printing jobs, such as standard business cards or flyers, can be completed the same day or within 24 hours depending on the finishing required.

Can you match specific Pantone colors?

Digital presses use CMYK toners/inks. While we can closely simulate most Pantone colors, for exact Pantone matching, we recommend our offset printing service.

What file format do you accept?

We prefer high-resolution (300dpi) PDF files with a 3mm bleed and crop marks. We can also work with AI, EPS, PSD, and high-res JPEG/TIFF files.

FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between acrylic and metal 3D letters?

Acrylic is lighter, more affordable, and available in any color. Metal (stainless steel or aluminum) is more durable, premium-looking, and ideal for outdoor use in Riyadh's climate.

How long do 3D signs last?

Our acrylic 3D signs last 5-7 years outdoors. Metal signs can last 10+ years. Illuminated signs come with a 2-3 year warranty on LEDs and power supplies.

Can I use my own logo?

Absolutely. Send us your logo in AI, PDF, or EPS format and we'll convert it into dimensional letters. We can also recreate logos from images or sketches.

Do you handle installation?

Yes, installation is included in our Premium and Custom packages. Our team covers all of Riyadh and ensures your sign is perfectly aligned and securely mounted.

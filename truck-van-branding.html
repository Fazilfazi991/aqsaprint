Built for Commercial Hard Use

Commercial vehicles take a beating — motorway speeds, dust, diesel fumes, and frequent washing. Standard vinyl doesn't survive that environment. We use heavy-duty commercial-grade cast vinyl with overlaminates rated for outdoor commercial use, ensuring your brand stays vivid and intact through years of hard work.

Every truck and van wrap includes reinforced edges at high-stress areas like door seams and wheel arches, with extra lamination on corners that are prone to peeling.

FAQ

Common Questions About Fleet Branding

How long does a van wrap take to install?

A standard delivery van takes 1–2 days. Larger trucks and trailers typically take 2–3 days. We schedule vehicles in rotation so your fleet stays operational throughout the project.

Can you brand vehicles of different sizes consistently?

Absolutely. This is our speciality. We design a master brand template that adapts to each vehicle's specific dimensions, ensuring your logo, colours and messaging are perfectly proportioned on every vehicle.

Do I get a discount for multiple vehicles?

Yes. We offer significant volume discounts for fleets of 5 or more vehicles. The more vehicles, the better the per-unit price. Contact us for a customised fleet quote.

Can you replace just one vehicle's branding if it's damaged?

Yes. We keep the master design files for all our fleet clients. Replacing a single vehicle is quick and the new wrap will match the rest of your fleet perfectly.

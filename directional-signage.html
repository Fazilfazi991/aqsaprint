Why Directional Signage Matters

In large offices, shopping malls, hospitals, and campuses, people need to find their way quickly. Poor wayfinding creates frustration, wastes time, and leaves a negative impression.

AQSA Print designs and installs complete wayfinding systems — from floor directories to corridor arrows to parking lot signs — all branded to your identity and built for Riyadh's climate.

  • Improves traffic flow and efficiency

FAQ

Common Questions

What size facility can you handle?

We design wayfinding systems for single offices to multi-building campuses. Recent projects include 50,000+ sqm malls and 10-floor office towers.

Do you offer bilingual signs?

Yes. All of our directional signs can include Arabic and English text. We follow Saudi signage standards for font size, placement, and reading order.

Can signs be updated later?

Absolutely. We offer modular directory systems with replaceable inserts so tenant names or room numbers can be updated without replacing the entire sign.

How long does installation take?

A typical office suite (10-20 signs) takes 1-2 days. Large facilities are phased over weeks to minimize disruption to operations.

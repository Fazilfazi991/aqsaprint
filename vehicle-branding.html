Frequently Asked Questions

How long does vehicle branding last?

Our premium vinyl wraps typically last 3 to 5 years depending on maintenance and sun exposure.

Will the wrap damage my vehicle's paint?

No, it actually protects the original paint from UV rays. It can be safely removed without residue.

Do I need RTA approval?

Yes, for commercial branding. We assist you with the necessary documentation.

How long does installation take?

Car branding takes 1-2 days. Vans and trucks 2-4 days depending on coverage.

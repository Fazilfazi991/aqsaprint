FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does vehicle branding last?

Our premium cast vinyl wraps last 3–5 years depending on maintenance and sun exposure. We recommend hand washing and shaded parking to maximise lifespan.

Will the wrap damage my paint?

No — in fact it protects it. Premium cast vinyl shields paint from UV, dust and minor scratches. When removed correctly, paint is preserved in original condition.

Can you brand any type of vehicle?

Yes. Cars, SUVs, vans, trucks, buses, trailers, boats and specialty vehicles. If it has a surface, we can wrap it with custom graphics.

Do you offer fleet discounts?

Absolutely. We offer significant volume pricing for 5+ vehicles. The more vehicles, the better the per-unit rate. Contact us for a custom fleet quote.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need RTA approval for signage?

Yes, most outdoor commercial signage requires RTA approval in Riyadh. We handle the entire documentation and submission process for you — it's included in our Business and Enterprise packages.

How long does signage last outdoors?

Our outdoor signage is built with UV-resistant and weatherproof materials designed to last 5-7 years in Riyadh's climate. We offer warranties up to 3 years for complete peace of mind.

Can you install at my location?

Absolutely. Our certified installation team covers all of Riyadh and surrounding areas. We handle mounting, electrical connections for illuminated signs, and full site cleanup.

What file do you need for design?

We work with AI, PDF, EPS, or even hand-drawn sketches. If you don't have a design, our in-house designers can create one for you at no extra charge when you book a package.

How fast can you deliver?

Standard turnaround is 5-10 days depending on complexity. Rush orders can be completed in as little as 3 days. Contact us and we'll confirm based on your specific project.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. We offer annual maintenance contracts to keep your signage looking brand new — including cleaning, bulb replacement, and structural checks.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I own the rights to the design?

Yes. Once the final invoice is paid, complete ownership and copyright of the logo or artwork is transferred to you. We provide all original vector source files (Ai/EPS).

How many revisions do I get?

Our standard design packages include 3 rounds of revisions on the chosen concept. We find this is usually more than enough to perfect the design while keeping the project moving efficiently.

I already have a logo, can you just fix it?

Yes. We offer "logo vectorization" services where we take your blurry, pixelated JPG logo and painstakingly redraw it as a crisp, scalable vector file so it can be printed on large signs.

Do you provide domain and hosting for websites?

We focus on the design and coding of the website. We will assist you in purchasing your own domain and hosting (so you maintain full ownership), and we will deploy the code to your server.

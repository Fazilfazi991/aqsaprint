FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For a basic shell-scheme upgrade (just wall graphics), we need 3-5 days. For custom fabricated wooden stalls, we recommend starting the process 3-4 weeks before the exhibition buildup date.

Do you handle the venue approvals?

Yes. If your stall requires structural modifications or electrical work, we can provide the necessary technical drawings and materials specifications required for venue management approval.

What happens after the exhibition?

We handle the teardown process. If you purchased modular hardware or 3D signage, we will pack it safely for you to reuse at future events. Temporary structures and vinyls will be disposed of.

Can you print graphics for a booth built by someone else?

Absolutely. If you already have a contractor building your booth structure, we can act solely as your large-format print partner to produce the wall graphics, floor decals, and signs.

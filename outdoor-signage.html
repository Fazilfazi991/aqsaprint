FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a permit for outdoor signage?

Yes, most outdoor commercial signs require RTA approval. We handle the entire permit application process, including drawings, specifications, and submissions.

How long will my outdoor sign last?

Our outdoor signs are rated for 5-7 years of continuous exposure. With proper maintenance, many signs last even longer. We offer warranties up to 3 years.

Can my sign be illuminated?

Absolutely. We offer LED backlighting, front-lighting, and edge-lighting options. Our LEDs are IP65-rated for dust and water resistance, perfect for Saudi conditions.

What happens during a sandstorm?

Our signs are designed to withstand sand and dust. Sealed LED modules, protected electrical connections, and durable face materials ensure your sign keeps working.

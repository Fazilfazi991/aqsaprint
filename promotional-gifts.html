Advanced Branding Techniques

A great gift is ruined by a cheap, peeling logo. We use the right branding technology for the specific material of the product you choose.

For metal items like pens and thermal flasks, we use precision fiber laser engraving that etches your logo permanently into the surface. For plastics and flat surfaces, we use UV printing for raised, full-color, scratch-resistant graphics. For fabrics, we offer premium embroidery or DTF printing for vibrant, flexible designs.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on the item. For high-end luxury gifts (like executive gift boxes), the MOQ can be as low as 10-20 units. For low-cost giveaways like basic pens or keychains, the MOQ is typically 500-1000 units.

How long does production take?

If the items are currently in local stock in Riyadh, branding and delivery usually takes 5-7 working days. If we need to source custom items internationally, it may take 2-4 weeks.

Can you create custom gift boxes?

Yes! Our most popular service is custom "kitting". We can manufacture a custom-printed hard cardboard presentation box with custom-cut foam inserts holding a combination of a branded pen, notebook, and flask.

Do you offer eco-friendly gifts?

Yes, we have a dedicated range of sustainable corporate gifts made from bamboo, cork, recycled wheat straw plastics, and organic cotton.
